What are the issues with existing services and the difficulties in advancing with AI?

Why are existing services inefficient?

Limitation of Real-time Analysis

  • Limited ability to support real-time data collection, analysis and rapid decision-making regarding market changes.
  • Difficulty in rapidly launching financial products or immediately responding to risks.

High Human Dependency

  • Many tasks like data collection, analysis, and risk management rely on human effort, leading to a manual process that consumes significant time and cost.
  • Customer response tasks that require 24/7 processing result in long waiting times.

Why is the AI service advancement process difficult?

Limitations in Securing AI Training Data and Data Connection

  • Existing data is often fragmented and unrefined, making it unsuitable for AI training.
  • Limitations exist in connecting and utilizing massive data for generating meaningful insights, restricting the provision of customized services.

High Setup and Hiring Costs

  • Server costs for establishing the AI training and development environment, and security system setup costs are incurred.
  • High costs for processing and refining vast/unstructured data for AI training, and professional personnel hiring costs are incurred.

Conclusion: Inefficiency in rapid decision-making or real-time service provision, difficulty in deriving meaningful insights through data connection, and high setup costs are incurred.
